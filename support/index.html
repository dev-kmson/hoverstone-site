Getting started

I opened Hoverstone but don't see a window

Hoverstone lives in the menu bar at the top of your screen. Click its icon or press ⌃⇧T to open it. You can change the shortcut in Settings.

Is it meant to replace my browser?

No. It's for the sites you open many times a day. When you need your full browser, the button at the end of the address bar opens the same page there.

Privacy and security

Does Hoverstone save my passwords?

No. It has no password manager and doesn't sync with other browsers. Sign-ins stay inside Hoverstone, separate from your main browser, and Wipe clears them whenever you like.

Is any of my data sent anywhere?

No. Nothing is sent to us. The Privacy Policy has the details.

Pro and purchases

What's the difference between the free version and Pro?

Everything for everyday use is free. Pro adds:

  • Float Several Pages at Once
  • Save Floating Windows to Home
  • Lock
  • Hide from Screen Sharing
  • Auto Wipe
  • Per-App Shortcuts
  • Send Copied Text
  • Hide Menu Bar Icon

How does the free trial work?

Start it in Settings to use every Pro feature for 14 days. When it ends, Pro features turn off until you buy Pro. You're never charged automatically.

I bought Pro on another Mac

Sign in with the same Apple Account, then choose Restore Purchase in Settings.

How do I get a refund?

Payments are handled by Apple. You can request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.
